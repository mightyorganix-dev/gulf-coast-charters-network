## What not to advertise

Do **not** hero, buy keywords for, or put above the fold:

- Party boats / walk-on seats (~$125)
- Semi-private / shared seats (~$69–$235)
- Jet ski, parasail, dolphin “cheap ticket” volume
- “Cheapest charter,” Groupon-style, or Hudson-style affordable-family framing
- OTA/Viator/GYG as primary demand (public takes ~20–30% destroy premier math) — **later & capped only**
- Demo/seed ratings or fictional operators as social proof

**Unit-math reason:** under Base assumptions (12% take, 4% weather drag, Stripe on fee slice, ~$8 support, ~$15 blended CAC), shared/party seats go **negative** on contribution. They drag blend toward the Excel Base $750 AOV and away from $900–950.
