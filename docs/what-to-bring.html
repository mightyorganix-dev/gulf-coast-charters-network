What to bring

Arrive light, arrive early, arrive ready — the ceremonial checklist behind a calm dock morning.

Everyone

  • Photo ID matching guest lead
  • Reef-safe sunscreen & polarized sunglasses
  • Light layer / windbreaker
  • Non-slip shoes or reliable sandals
  • Water & light snacks (no glass)
  • Soft cooler for fillets / keepsakes

Fishing days

  • Alabama saltwater license (unless included)
  • Cash/card for mate tip
  • Motion-sickness plan if needed
  • Hat with a strap

Spearfishing

  • Dive cert card
  • Mask/fins if preferred personal gear
  • Optional gun rental noted in booking

Sunset / private

  • BYOB only if captain approved
  • Light celebration details shared 48h prior
  • Camera — the horizon is the product
