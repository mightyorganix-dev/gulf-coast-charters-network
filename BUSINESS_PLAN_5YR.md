# Gulf Coast Charters Network — 5-Year Business Plan

**Entity / brand:** Gulf Coast Charters Network (Mighty Organix)  
**Market:** Alabama Gulf Coast — Gulf Shores 36542, Orange Beach 36561, Fort Morgan, Perdido  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Status:** Planning document — figures mix **sourced market context** and explicitly labeled **ASSUMPTIONS**

---

## 1. Executive summary

Gulf Coast Charters Network (GCCN) is a **premier multi-operator outdoor activities hub** for the Alabama Gulf Coast. Guest experience is inspired by best-in-class marina hubs (e.g., Zeke’s Landing density and discovery) without cloning them; operations and data follow an owned **Items / Resources / Channels** model with **Stripe deposits** — avoiding permanent FareHarbor ~6% platform dependency and avoiding OTA 20–30% economics as the brand’s primary channel.

The destination demand pool is large: Alabama’s Beaches (Gulf Shores, Orange Beach, Fort Morgan) drew roughly **6.49M visitors and $6.69B spending in 2024**, and about **7.14M visitors / $6.87B spending in 2025**, with lodging sales rising to a record **~$923M** in 2025 ([GSOBT 2024 handout](https://www.gulfshores.com/sites/default/files/2025-07/2024%20GSOB%20%26%20Baldwin%20Co.%20Economic%20Impact%20Handout.pdf); [Fox10 Sep 2026](https://www.fox10tv.com/2026/09/06/new-tourism-impact-numbers-gulf-shores-orange-beach/); [al.com Mar 2026](https://www.al.com/news/2026/03/alabama-beaches-set-tourism-record-in-2025-with-923-million-in-lodging-rentals.html)). Seasonality is sharp: roughly **70% of Baldwin lodging tax** falls in **April–September** (Alabama Tourism Economic Report, as summarized in industry commentary).

**Recommended primary model:** **Asset-light marketplace** collecting a **12% network commission (Base)** on gross merchandise value (GMV) for partner-operated trips booked through GCCN’s owned Stripe stack (deposit + balance), plus modest **sister-brand / selective owned inventory** margin on spearfishing and curated experiences. Target take-rate band **10–15%**. OTAs used later only as inventory **Channels**.

**Year 1 focus:** Prove direct-book unit economics and quiet-luxury CX with a tight operator roster.  
**Year 5 ambition:** Multi-marina corridor platform with durable direct share, spear cross-sell, and optional white-label readiness — without becoming a discount aggregator.

## 2. Business model (recommended path)

### Primary recommendation: Marketplace commission + selective owned SKUs (hybrid-light)

| Element | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary P&L engine** | **10–15% commission on GMV** (Base case **12%**) on partner bookings via owned web + Stripe |
| **Deposit** | Guest pays deposit online (ASSUMPTION **25–40%** of trip price); balance per operator rules |
| **Payment processing** | Stripe fees (~**2.9% + $0.30** ASSUMPTION) modeled as COGS against take-rate revenue *or* passed through — Base: **platform absorbs processing on its fee slice**; see Assumptions sheet |
| **Owned / sister inventory** | Gulf Coast Spearfishing + optional limited watersports — higher contribution margin; still CX-curated |
| **OTA** | Optional Channel at **20–30%** third-party take — **not** primary; inventory-capped |
| **Partner referral** | Marina/lodging partners at **ASSUMPTION 5–8%** referral credit (below OTA) |

**Why this path**
- Aligns with premier positioning (control UX, weather honesty, itinerary).
- Captures more economics than FareHarbor’s customer-facing ~6% while remaining attractive vs Viator/GYG ~20–30% ([Roverd](https://www.roverd.com/fareharbor-pricing/); [OTA Playbook](https://otaplaybook.com/ota-commission-models/)).
- Avoids heavy CapEx of owning a large fishing fleet in Year 1–2.
- Scales via Items/Resources/Channels software leverage.

### Alternatives (not primary)

| Alternative | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pure owner-operator fleet** | Full margin, brand control | CapEx, insurance, weather risk concentration, slow scale |
| **Hybrid owner + network (heavy owned)** | Vertical integration | Dilutes capital; ops complexity early |
| **FareHarbor-dependent desk** | Fast go-live | Permanent ~6%+; weaker data ownership; API fees on OTA |
| **OTA-first marketplace** | Traffic | Brand = commodity; 20–30% bleed; conflicts with quiet luxury |

## 4. Revenue streams

1. **Network commission** on partner GMV (core).  
2. **Owned / sister-brand trip margin** (spear, selective experiences).  
3. **Partner referral / marina desk fees** (optional).  
4. **Ancillary:** photo packages, premium itinerary concierge, gift cards (**ASSUMPTION** small %).  
5. **Future:** SaaS / white-label subscription for marina desks (**Y3+ option** — not in Base GMV until gated).

## 6. Risk / weather / insurance / regulatory

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| **Weather / tropical** | Green/Yellow/Red guest protocol; flexible rebook; deposit policies clear; hurricane season cash reserve |
| **Seasonality** | ~70% lodging tax Apr–Sep — build shoulder products (fall fishing, sunset); control winter burn |
| **Insurance** | Require operator COIs; network E&O/cyber; no booking without verified coverage |
| **USCG / ADCNR / federal seasons** | Credential checks; red snapper / reef season literacy in CX scripts |
| **Reputation** | Curate supply; fire underperformers; review response SLA |
| **Platform dependency** | Own Stripe + site; OTAs optional Channels only |
| **Competitive marina SEO** | Differentiate on quiet luxury + multi-marina + itinerary ops, not clone Zeke’s |
| **Liability events** | Contracts, waivers, partner standards, incident playbooks |

## 7. Annual P&L scenarios (Y1–Y5)

All dollars **USD**; **ASSUMPTION-driven** operating model. GMV = guest trip value booked through GCCN. Platform revenue ≈ take rate × GMV (+ small owned margin). **Net profit** = platform revenue − OpEx − variable COGS.

### Scenario definitions
- **Conservative:** Slower operator onboarding, higher CAC, 10% take rate, softer utilization.  
- **Base:** 12% + 1.5pts owned/sister (13.5% effective), disciplined direct mix, founder-lean OpEx.  
- **Upside:** Faster density, 17% effective take (owned mix), strong repeat, efficient CAC.

Detailed monthly Y1 and formula engine: `data/GCCN_5YR_Financial_Model.xlsx`.

### Summary — Platform net profit

**Point estimates** from `data/GCCN_5YR_Financial_Model.xlsx` (edit Assumptions to stress-test):

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Y1 | **~-$53k** | **~-$13k** | **~+$39k** |
| Y2 | **~-$54k** | **~+$19k** | **~+$130k** |
| Y3 | **~-$51k** | **~+$77k** | **~+$290k** |
| Y4 | **~-$43k** | **~+$176k** | **~+$566k** |
| Y5 | **~-$26k** | **~+$343k** | **~+$1.04M** |

**Report anchors (Base) — sensitivity ranges around the model:**  
- **Year 1 Base net profit range: roughly -$15,000 to +$10,000** (model point ~**-$13k**; near break-even / lean investment year).  
- **Year 5 Base net profit range: roughly +$280,000 to +$480,000** (model point ~**+$343k**).

*Ranges reflect ± sensitivity on AOV, take rate, CAC, and OpEx. Conservative stays challenged through Y5 under soft demand — cut burn or raise density. Stripe is modeled on the **platform fee slice**, not full GMV.*
