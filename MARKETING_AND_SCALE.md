## 2. ICP / guest personas

### Persona A — “Quiet Luxury Family” (primary)
- **Who:** Couples / multi-gen families (ages ~35–65), Midwestern & Southeast fly/drive; stay 4–7 nights in condo/hotel.
- **Job to be done:** One memorable private or semi-private day on the water without marina chaos or coupon-feel booking.
- **Triggers:** Anniversary, reunion, “first Gulf trip with kids,” red-snapper season buzz.
- **Wants:** Curated captains, clear itinerary, weather honesty (Green/Yellow/Red), deposit clarity, photo moments, dock calm.
- **Won’t tolerate:** Bait-and-switch pricing, overcrowded party decks, pushy upsells, opaque cancellation.

### Persona B — “Serious Angler / Spearo”
- **Who:** Credential-aware anglers and spearfishing guests (sister brand).
- **Job:** Species/season match, safety, captain fit — not cheapest seat.
- **Channel:** SEO (“Orange Beach private offshore,” “Gulf Coast spearfishing”), referral, tournament adjacency.

### Persona C — “Celebration / Sunset Buyout”
- **Who:** Proposals, birthdays, small corporate offsites.
- **Job:** Private sunset / pontoon / yacht buyout with white-glove messaging and itinerary PDF.
- **AOV:** Higher; fewer price shoppers.

### Persona D — “Watersports Impulse” (secondary, volume)
- **Who:** Beach week day-trippers wanting jet ski / parasail / dolphin.
- **Role:** Awareness + review flywheel; protect brand with curated partners only (no race-to-bottom desk).

### Operator ICP (supply-side)
- USCG-credentialed, insured, review-strong six-packs and specialty boats who want **direct bookings** without forever FareHarbor % or 20–30% OTA cuts — and who will honor quiet-luxury CX standards.

## 4. Channel mix

**Priority order (Y1):**

1. **Direct web (primary)** — Vercel production + Stripe Checkout deposits; GitHub Pages as preview only.
2. **SEO / content** — Corridor + category pages (private offshore, inshore, sunset, spear, jet ski); comparison-intent without discount tone; itinerary / what-to-bring / safety hubs.
3. **Google Business Profile** — Network + key marina partnership locations; review velocity.
4. **Marina / lodging partnerships** — Concierge cards, QR to direct book (prefer partner referral fee **below** OTA rates — ASSUMPTION 5–8% partner credit).
5. **Meta + Google Ads** — High-intent search first; Meta retargeting for abandoners and lookalikes of bookers.
6. **Email / SMS** — Deposit confirm → itinerary → weather status → review ask → anniversary win-back.
7. **OTA (later)** — Viator/GYG as **Channel** only after direct funnel is healthy; rate-fence and inventory-cap.

**Do not lead with:** coupon sites, Groupon-style deals, “cheapest charter” creative.

## 5. Funnel

```
Awareness → Consider → Book → Deposit (Stripe) → Pre-trip itinerary
    → Day-of weather/status → Experience → Review → Repeat / Referral
```

| Stage | Assets | Success metric |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | SEO, GBP, Meta/Google, marina QR, lodging partners | Qualified sessions, branded search |
| Consider | Trips / captains / safety / what-to-bring / FAQ | Trip-page → book CTR |
| Book | `book.html` + live availability | Checkout starts |
| Deposit | Stripe Checkout (partial deposit) | Paid deposits / abandoned recovery |
| Itinerary | Guest itinerary + SMS templates | Open rate / confirmation replies |
| Experience | Weather Green/Yellow/Red honesty | On-time ops, weather rebooks |
| Review | Post-trip ask (GBP + site) | Review rate, NPS/CSAT |
| Repeat | Email/SMS 10–11 months later; spear cross-sell | Repeat book rate, LTV |

## 6. 90-day launch marketing calendar

### Days 0–30 — Foundation
- Production domain on Vercel; Stripe live deposits; analytics (GA4 + conversion events).
- GBP claim/optimize; NAP consistency for corridor ZIPs.
- Operator onboarding wave 1 (target **ASSUMPTION 8–12** live Items with calendars).
- Photo/video brand kit (boats, water, dock calm — not coupon creative).
- SEO scaffolding: category + marina-corridor landing pages.
- Concierge kit PDF for 5–10 lodging/marina partners.
- Email/SMS transactional templates only (no blast yet).

### Days 31–60 — Demand ignition
- Google Search Ads: private charter / sunset / jet ski intent (exact + phrase).
- Meta: retarget site visitors; lookalike from deposit completers (once pixel has volume).
- Soft launch PR / DMO familiarization with GSOBT-style tourism contacts (relationship, not spam).
- Sister-brand bar live: spear cross-sell module on relevant trips.
- First review campaign for completed trips.
- Weekly operator office hours (availability hygiene, photo QA).

### Days 61–90 — Proof & scale readiness
- Double down on winning search terms; pause vanity keywords.
- Publish 2–3 “quiet luxury” guest stories (with permission).
- Partner referral tracking (UTM + promo codes).
- Shoulder-season packaging (fall fishing / sunset) before winter trough.
- Decide OTA **Channel** pilot (inventory-capped) or defer — default **defer** if direct CAC is healthy.
- 90-day KPI readout → feed Y1 Base plan.

## 7. Scalability

### Items / Resources / Channels (FareHarbor-inspired, owned)
- **Items:** Bookable products (4hr private offshore, sunset buyout, spear day, jet ski hour…).
- **Resources:** Boats, captains, docks, gear sets — capacity & conflict rules.
- **Channels:** Direct web, partner referral, ads, (later) OTA — each with take-rate and allotment.

**Growth path**
1. Deepen Orange Beach / Gulf Shores roster quality over quantity.
2. Add Fort Morgan + Perdido inventory with same CX bar.
3. Multi-marina calendars + unified guest itinerary.
4. Sister **Gulf Coast Spearfishing** cross-sell and shared CRM.
5. Optional later: **white-label / franchise** marina desk software for other Gulf corridors — only after local unit economics proven (Y3+ hypothesis).
