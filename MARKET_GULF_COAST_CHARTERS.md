## Trip categories
Offshore · Inshore · Spearfishing · Dolphin · Sunset · Jet ski / watersports · Private yacht

## Competitive insight
- Party boats & shared seats compete on price; premier guests buy privacy, coaching, weather honesty, and dock calm.
- Owner-operator 6-packs with deep review graphs set the emotional bar (family-first private fishing).
- Network value = discovery + trust layer + operator tools (manifests, SMS, weather calls) — not undercutting marina offices.
