# Captain outreach — high-AOV path

**Brand:** Gulf Coast Charters Network  
**You:** Mighty Organix (speak as yourself)  
**Offer:** Premier network. We send **private / yacht / spear / celebration** guests. You keep the boat, the guest, and most of the money.

**Economics to say out loud (do not hide):**
- Guest books on our site and pays a **deposit** (about 30%) on Stripe.
- **Network fee: 12%** of the trip price (we handle booking, itinerary, weather texts, reviews).
- You keep **~88%**. Compare: FareHarbor ~6% forever + OTA 20–30% if they send the guest.
- No monthly software fee. No exclusive lock in Year 1. You can keep your own phone book.

**Who we want first (say no to the rest):**
- USCG-credentialed, insured, review-strong 6-packs and specialty boats
- Private offshore / private inshore / sunset / yacht / spearfishing
- Captains who will honor weather honesty and quiet-luxury CX
- **Not** first-wave: cheap party-boat volume, Groupon, “cheapest seat” operators

---

## 1. In person (dock / marina) — 90 seconds

**Open (handshake, no pitch-deck energy)**  
“Hey Captain — I’m building Gulf Coast Charters Network. Not another coupon desk. We send **private and celebration trips** — offshore, inshore, sunset, yacht, spear. I wanted to meet you before I list anyone.”

**Why they should care**  
“Guests book on our site, pay a deposit, get a real itinerary and weather call. You show up to a guest who already knows the plan. We’re not taking your walk-ups. We’re filling **premium days** you already want.”

**The number (plain)**  
“We take **12%**. You keep the rest. No monthly fee. Not exclusive. OTAs take 20–30% — we’re not that.”

**The ask**  
“If your insurance and ticket are current, I’d like to list **one** signature trip with you — your best private day — and send you two or three real inquiries this season. Fair?”

**If they stall**  
“No paperwork today. I’ll text/email a one-pager. You decide. If it’s not a fit, we’re done — I only want captains who want this kind of guest.”

**Close**  
“What’s the best email or text for the one-pager? And what’s your best private trip — hours, boat, max people, from-price?”

**Do not say**
- “We’ll make you famous” / “guaranteed bookings”
- “Cheapest on the beach”
- “We’re like Zeke’s / FareHarbor”
- Anything that sounds like they work *for* you

---

## 2. If they have 3 more minutes — talk track

1. **Guest standard:** Once-in-a-lifetime feel, not seat-count. Weather Green / Yellow / Red. Deposit credit on Red.  
2. **What we do:** Site, deposit, itinerary, guest SMS, review ask.  
3. **What you do:** Run the trip to your standard. We don’t captain your boat.  
4. **Who we send:** Families, celebrations, serious anglers/spearos — **$900–2,500+ days**, not $125 walk-ons.  
5. **Proof:** Show the live site on your phone. Point at private / sunset / spear cards.

## 7. Objection handles

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| “I already have FareHarbor.” | “Keep it. We’re another **direct** door. 12% only when we send the guest — no monthly.” |
| “12% is high.” | “OTAs take 20–30% and own the guest. We own less of you. You keep ~88% and the relationship.” |
| “I don’t need more bookings.” | “Then we’re a bad fit — I’m looking for captains who want **better** days, not more cheap seats.” |
| “Will you steal my guests?” | “We send *our* guests to you. Your walk-ups stay yours. No exclusive.” |
| “I hate computers.” | “You won’t run software. We book, you get a text/email packet. Show up and run the trip.” |
| “What’s the catch?” | “We curate. If CX or weather honesty slips, we stop sending. That’s the catch — quality both ways.” |

---

## 8. Dock visit checklist (print)

- [ ] Boat / name / marina / slip  
- [ ] Captain legal name + phone + email  
- [ ] Best private trip + from-price + hours + capacity  
- [ ] USCG + insurance current? (yes / need docs)  
- [ ] Days they want Network bookings  
- [ ] Permission to list (verbal is enough to start; written confirm by email)  
- [ ] Send confirmation email same day  

**First 8–12 captains to hunt:** private offshore 6-packs, private inshore family boats, sunset/yacht, spearfishing. Skip volume party boats until the premier roster is full.
