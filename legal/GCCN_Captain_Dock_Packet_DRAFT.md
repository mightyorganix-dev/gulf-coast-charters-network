- Insurance waivers that pretend GCCN is the vessel operator (you are **not** the captain)

# FORM A — Operator Participation Agreement (Independent Contractor)

**DRAFT — counsel review required before relying on this.**

This Agreement is between **Gulf Coast Charters Network / Mighty Organix** (“Network”) and the undersigned operator (“Operator”).

**1. Relationship.** Operator is an **independent contractor**, not an employee, partner, or agent for employment purposes. Operator captains their own vessel and controls how trips are run (within law and this Agreement). Network provides a booking marketplace, guest deposit collection, itinerary/comms tools, and guest matching.

**2. Non-exclusive.** Operator may book guests from any other source. Network does not require exclusivity in Year 1 under this draft.

**3. Network fee.** On bookings made through Network for listed trips, Network’s fee is **twelve percent (12%)** of the listed trip price (GMV). Operator receives the remainder according to the payout process Network uses (Stripe or otherwise), less any agreed refunds/credits under the weather/cancel policy. No monthly software fee under this draft.

**4. Deposits.** Guests typically pay a deposit (**about 30%** unless Form B says otherwise) via Network’s Stripe checkout. Deposit float is **not** Operator’s to spend until Network’s payout rules say so. Balance collection may be Network or Operator as stated on Form B.

**5. Listings.** Operator authorizes Network to list the trip(s) on Form B with the stated price, photos Operator provides or approves, and availability Operator controls (calendar). Operator will keep prices and availability accurate.

**6. Credentials & insurance.** Before any live guest booking, Operator must maintain: (a) required **USCG** credential for the vessel/operation; (b) **liability insurance** with Network listed as interested party / additional insured **if counsel so directs** (confirm with insurer); (c) vessel documentation/registration as required. Operator will notify Network within **24 hours** if coverage lapses or credentials are suspended.

**7. Safety & compliance.** Operator is solely responsible for vessel safety, crew, guests aboard, fisheries rules, and on-water conduct. Network does not operate the vessel.

**8. Guest standard.** Operator will deliver a premier private-charter experience consistent with Network’s positioning (no bait-and-switch to a different boat/trip without guest consent). Weather: honor Network’s **Green / Yellow / Red** guest protocol; on **Red** cancels, guest deposit is credited/rebooked per published policy (Form B).

**9. Cancellations.** Operator no-shows or Operator-caused cancels: Network may refund the guest and withhold/recover Network fee and reasonable costs from amounts otherwise due Operator. Guest no-shows: per Form B.

**10. Reviews & marks.** Operator grants Network a limited license to use Operator’s business name, boat name, and approved photos for listing and marketing. Network grants Operator a limited license to say they are listed on GCCN. Either party may revoke publicity rights on written notice for future use.

**11. Term.** Starts on the Effective Date below. Either party may end participation with **14 days’ written notice** (email/text OK). Bookings already confirmed remain Operator’s duty to complete unless guest agrees otherwise.

**12. Limitation (draft).** To the fullest extent allowed by law, Network’s liability to Operator under this draft is limited to fees Network actually received on Operator’s Network bookings in the prior 3 months. This is a **placeholder — counsel must rewrite.**

**13. Governing law (draft placeholder).** State of Alabama. Venue TBD by counsel.
